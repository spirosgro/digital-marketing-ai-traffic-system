# Privacy and AI governance

## Marketing data

- Document purpose, lawful basis, recipients, retention, and data-subject rights.
- Gate optional analytics and advertising tags according to the applicable consent design.
- Keep public-business outreach rules under legal review for each jurisdiction.
- Use aggregate exports in this repository. Do not publish contact-level CRM data.
- Separate marketing identities from operational traffic telemetry.

## Product claims and AI governance

- Classify the real use case under the EU AI Act with qualified legal and technical review.
- Maintain intended purpose, operating boundaries, human oversight, logs, test evidence,
  failure modes, and change history.
- Apply the NIST AI RMF functions Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage as an operational checklist.
- Use simulation and bounded pilots before any live optimisation.
- Require safe fallback and accountable human intervention.

## Evidence status

| Label | Meaning | Marketing use |
|---|---|---|
| Hypothesis | Untested proposition | Internal planning only |
| Simulated | Produced from synthetic or modelled data | Clearly labelled example |
| Pilot observed | Measured in a bounded pilot | State scope and uncertainty |
| Independently verified | Reviewed by an independent party | Cite method and verifier |
