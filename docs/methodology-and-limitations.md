# Methodology and limitations

## Method

The project reframes an academic digital-marketing presentation as a public portfolio case.
It maps a municipal buying committee, defines a pilot-oriented funnel, creates a synthetic
campaign dataset, calculates transparent performance metrics, and proposes a constrained
budget allocation. Governance artefacts distinguish marketing data from traffic-system data.

## Assumptions

- Currency is euro.
- A qualified lead meets documented organisational, problem, timing, and role criteria.
- An opportunity has an operational owner and a plausible procurement route.
- A pilot commitment is a documented intention, not recognised revenue.
- Attribution is last-known campaign for the example and does not prove causation.

## Limitations

The synthetic dataset does not represent market benchmarks. The score is sensitive to the
chosen weights and the small number of channels. The example does not calculate incremental
lift, lifetime value, tender probability, safety impact, or production AI performance.
Jurisdiction-specific procurement, ePrivacy, and direct-marketing rules require specialist
advice. The project does not validate an actual traffic-control system.
