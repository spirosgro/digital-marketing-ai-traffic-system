# KPI framework

| Stage | KPI | Formula | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reach | CTR | clicks / impressions | Message-channel response, not buying intent |
| Engagement | Engagement rate | engaged sessions / landing sessions | Content usefulness proxy |
| Qualification | Lead conversion | qualified leads / landing sessions | Fit and readiness signal |
| Pipeline | Opportunity conversion | opportunities / qualified leads | Buying-committee progression |
| Pilot | Pilot conversion | pilot commitments / opportunities | Commercial and procurement progress |
| Efficiency | Cost per qualified lead | spend / qualified leads | Early-stage acquisition efficiency |
| Efficiency | Cost per opportunity | spend / opportunities | Pipeline efficiency |
| Efficiency | Cost per pilot | spend / pilot commitments | Illustrative pilot acquisition cost |

## Channel score

The example score combines engagement (20%), lead efficiency (25%), opportunity efficiency
(25%), strategic fit (20%), and data confidence (10%). Budget shares are constrained to
10% to 30% per channel. The score is a transparent heuristic, not a causal model or autonomous
optimisation system.

## Guardrails

No budget increase is approved solely from a score. Review sample size, data completeness,
channel saturation, procurement stage, legal basis, and evidence quality. Run controlled
experiments where practical and maintain a written decision record.
