# Evidence-led value proposition

## Outcome statement

Support a municipality in testing whether adaptive traffic management can improve selected
mobility outcomes within a bounded pilot, with transparent baselines, safety constraints,
human override, and documented procurement evidence.

## Proof ladder

1. **Problem evidence:** local congestion and service reliability are defined with an approved baseline.
2. **Technical evidence:** interfaces, constraints, logging, and fallback operation are demonstrated.
3. **Pilot evidence:** pre-agreed measures are compared across a defined evaluation window.
4. **Operational evidence:** staff can monitor, override, recover, and explain the system.
5. **Scale evidence:** transfer conditions, cost, governance, and uncertainty are documented.

Marketing may describe a capability only when the claims register identifies the evidence
owner, scope, status, and expiry date. Synthetic figures in this repository are examples,
not claims about an existing product.
