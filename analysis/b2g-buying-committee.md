# B2G buying committee

| Role | Primary concern | Useful evidence | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mayor or executive sponsor | Public value and political accountability | Baseline, pilot outcome, citizen impact | Generic AI superlatives |
| Mobility director | Network performance and safe operations | Delay, reliability, intervention boundaries | Uncontrolled automation |
| IT and security | Integration, resilience, supportability | Architecture, logging, rollback, supplier controls | Hidden dependencies |
| Procurement | Fair competition and value for money | Clear scope, acceptance criteria, exit terms | Informal commitments |
| DPO and legal | Lawful processing and proportionality | Data map, DPIA trigger, retention | Purpose expansion |
| Finance | Cost, affordability, and risk | Pilot budget, TCO, sensitivity | Selective savings claims |

A qualified opportunity requires at least one operational owner, one accountable sponsor,
and documented access to procurement, privacy, and technical reviewers. An email response
alone is not a sales-qualified opportunity.
